# Data retention

Readable by everyone. This document states how long Northwind keeps records and who may ask for
them to be removed early.

## Order records

Order records are kept for seven years from the date of delivery, because they are part of the
financial record. A deletion request against an order record is refused for that reason, and the
refusal cites this paragraph.

## Support conversations

Support conversations are kept for eighteen months. A customer may ask for a conversation to be
removed sooner, and the request is honoured unless the conversation is attached to an open dispute.

## Telemetry

Request traces are kept for thirty days. Traces carry identifiers rather than message contents, so
a trace older than thirty days cannot be used to reconstruct what a customer said.

## Who decides

Retention periods are set by the data protection owner and reviewed annually. An individual team
may keep records for less time than this document states, and may not keep them for longer without
a recorded exception.
