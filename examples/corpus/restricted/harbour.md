# Project Harbour

Readable by the executive group, and **hidden**: a principal outside that group must not learn that
this document exists. That is the distinction PRD 6.4 turns into a rule — the system may not say
"there is a document you may not read", because the existence of a file named after an unannounced
acquisition is itself the disclosure.

## Status

Diligence on the Harbour target closed last week. The commercial terms are agreed in principle and
the announcement is held until the depot leases transfer.

## Why it is hidden rather than merely restricted

A restricted document may be known to exist. Somebody asking "do we have anything on Harbour" can be
told there is material they cannot read, and nothing has leaked. Here the name is the secret, so the
answer to that question has to be byte-identical to the answer given when nothing was found at all.

## Depots

The target operates three depots. Two overlap with Northwind's existing coverage and are expected to
close within a year of completion.
