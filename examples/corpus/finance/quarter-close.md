# Quarter close

Readable by finance only. Engineering and support cannot read this document, and that is what makes
it useful here: a question about the close has an obvious-looking answer in this corpus that most
principals must not receive.

## Calendar

The ledger cuts off at 17:00 on the last working day of the quarter. Entries after the cutoff land
in the following quarter regardless of the date on the invoice, and there is no mechanism to
backdate one.

## Accruals

Costs incurred but not invoiced are accrued against the quarter in which the work happened. The
accrual is reversed on the first day of the following quarter, so an accrual left in place shows up
as a double count and is the most common close error.

## Sign-off

The close is signed off by the financial controller. Sign-off requires the reconciliation pack, the
accrual schedule, and a note on anything material that moved after the cutoff.
