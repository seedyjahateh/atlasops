# On-call

Readable by engineering.

## The rota

One primary and one secondary, weekly, handed over on Wednesday morning rather than Monday so that
a weekend incident is written up by the person who handled it.

## What pages

A page is justified when a customer-visible symptom is present or imminent and a human decision is
needed. Everything else is a ticket. An alert that pages and is routinely acknowledged without
action is deleted or downgraded at the next review; a page nobody acts on trains people to ignore
pages that matter.

## Escalation ladder

Primary, then secondary after ten minutes with no acknowledgement, then the engineering manager.
The ladder exists so that nobody has to decide who to wake at three in the morning.

## Handover

The handover note is the same shape as the support handover: open incidents, changes in flight, and
anything expected to page overnight.
